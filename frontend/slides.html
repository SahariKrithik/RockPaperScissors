Rock Paper Scissors

A tiny game with a big streak obsession.

How to play

  • ✊ Pick a move - the computer picks one too.
  • 🔥 Every win grows your streak.
  • 💥 One loss and the streak is over.
  • 🤝 Draws are safe - your streak survives.

The leaderboard

🏆

When your streak ends, put your name on it. Only the 10 longest streaks ever make the board - how long can you last?

Under the hood

  • 🎨 A website with no framework - just HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • 🤖 A small Node.js server on Render picks the computer's move and decides every winner, so the browser can't cheat.
  • 🗄️ MongoDB Atlas remembers the leaderboard forever.
